What Purpose Created Channel

When working on homemade machines, toy tractors, and mechanics, enthusiasts face constant challenges: gear teeth stripping, weak motors, improper lubricants, and lack of clarity on what raw parts cost or how to implement fixes.

GG Creation (@GGCreative-x9o) was created with a clear mission: to build innovative homemade products from scratch, test real mechanical implementations (like toy tractor gearboxes and electric steering), and publish 100% honest spent time and material bills ("If Bill") so anyone in the community can learn, replicate, and innovate.

Homemade Tractor Builds

Handcrafting working scale tractors with custom steel sheet bodies, electric geared drive, and steering systems.

Gearbox Teardowns & Fixes

Solving real gearbox failures (e.g. John Deere toy tractors), upgrading to brass pinions, and formulating SV lubricants.

Real Cost Transparency

Every project includes an itemized bill showing exact costs of motors, sheet metal, gears, and materials.
